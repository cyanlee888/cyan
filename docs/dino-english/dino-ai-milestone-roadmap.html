Positioning · Who We Are

A real-time, interactive AI English class for children aged 4–13 in non-English-speaking countries — an AI avatar teacher delivers an English-learning experience where kids speak up and get feedback in every lesson, at about 1/10 the price of a live tutor.

Target Users

  • The buyer: parents who value their child's English education but are put off by the price or scheduling hassle of human tutors
  • The user: children aged 4–13
North Star · MPW
Meaningful Minutes per Week — real learning minutes, not vanity DAU

Differentiation · Why Us

Human 1-on-1 tutors1/10 the price, available anytime, no booking; the AI teacher has memory and consistency
Pre-recorded learning appsnot tap-through videos but real-time conversation — the child speaks and the teacher instantly responds, corrects, and encourages (Teaching Moves)
General AI chat productsa structured 864-lesson curriculum mapped to CEFR/GSE, plus placement tests and learning reports — a "course," not a "chat toy"

Moats

  • Proprietary teaching model: Focused on children's English learning and real-time interaction.
  • Classroom and learning data: Accumulated over time to support personalized teaching.
  • Curriculum and content assets: A leveled curriculum covering listening, speaking, reading and writing.
  • Pedagogical expertise: Reusable guidance, correction and feedback strategies.
Current Focus · Core Product Metrics

Current releases follow one shared journey: registration → first lesson started → lesson completed → payment after completion. These targets align how each stage is evaluated.
