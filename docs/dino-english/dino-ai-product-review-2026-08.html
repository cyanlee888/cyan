04 · Progress & Gaps

What worked was capability building; the next breakthrough is outcome convergence

The past six months strengthened product capabilities, key experiences, and the data foundation. The next stage should improve first learning, continued learning, and commercial conversion.

What worked

  • From-0-to-1 delivery cadence

    Completed learning, commerce, globalization, and data capabilities in about 11 weeks.

  • Key experience metrics improved

    First-lesson completion, D1, and registration completion improved overall across complete weeks, while technical issues began to be measured and fixed.

  • Globalization and brand migration foundations

    Multi-market login, product configuration, attribution, and local-payment readiness were established without breaking accounts or historical data during the rebrand.

  • From intuition to visible problems

    GA4, PostHog, AppsFlyer, and production orders now provide a combined operating view.

Where we still need a breakthrough

  • Feature breadth grew faster than learning outcomes

    Dino, Explore, Play, coins, and multiple touchpoints expanded, while first open to lesson completion remains 7.9%.

  • A continued-learning path is not yet established

    D1 is 12.2% and D7 is 2.4%; a stable path after the first lesson has not yet formed.

  • Parent value and commercial conversion remain weak

    First-lesson reports, learning plans, the paywall, and renewal value do not yet tell one clear and consistent parent-value story.

  • Key outcome metrics remain incomplete

    Technical exits, meaningful learning time, renewal rate, and LTV still lack reliable baselines.

05 · Top Opportunities

The three biggest opportunities: make value happen continuously

These opportunities address a child's first success, continued learning, and parent payment in sequence—the single value chain for the next stage.

First Lesson Success

Turn “start the first lesson” into “complete the first meaningful learning result”

  • Trigger permissions in context; fix weak-network and loading issues
  • Shorten the first lesson and continuously show progress and report value
  • Resolve ASR / VAD / prompt / template blockers
Seven-day Learning Loop

Build a seven-day loop children continue and parents understand

  • Keep one clear next learning task on the home page
  • Use the same evidence across reports, learning plans, and weekly summaries
  • Use Dino / Explore / Play for review and weak-skill practice
Lighthouse Market

Prove value, payment, and renewal in one lighthouse market

  • Clarify value through learning outcomes, plan benefits, and local pricing
  • Align product, channel, and operations in one focused market
  • Use real learning outcomes to strengthen trust in reports and the paywall

07 · Key Choices

Four key choices for the next stage

Clarify the primary user, lighthouse market, milestone cadence, and revenue target so resources stay focused on one value chain.

Primary user

Choose the age band, level, and parent need to serve first instead of optimizing for ages 4–13 at once.

Lighthouse market

Deeply validate one market from August to December while maintaining baseline readiness elsewhere.

Milestone cadence

Sequence new requirements and releases around each milestone and its core metrics, avoiding cross-stage dilution.

Revenue target & investment

Confirm the December $1m revenue target together with the required acquisition budget, paying-user scale, and renewal level.
