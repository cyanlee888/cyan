## Privacy at a Glance

Dino English is an English-learning app for children and teens.

**What we collect:** Your parent account details (name, email, country), your child's display name and age range, course selections, learning activity (course progress, lesson progress, scores, quiz answers, practice results, time spent on each activity, and interactions with lesson pages and buttons), teacher selections, voice recordings for pronunciation practice, AI tutor conversations, and basic device information. If we make available paid subscriptions, gamified learning, rewards, learning games, Dino Agent, learning reports, push notifications, check-in streaks, rankings, or other future features, we may also collect the information needed to provide those features, as described in this Policy.

**Why:** To create and manage accounts, deliver English-learning courses and activities, assess pronunciation, adapt the learning path to your child's level, support parent-facing learning progress features, operate paid features where available, and keep the App running smoothly and securely. If we make available Dino Agent, learning reports, task recommendations, or additional learning scenarios in the future, we may also use the information needed to provide those features, as described in this Policy.

**Who can see it:** We share data with service providers who help us operate the app (cloud hosting, speech recognition, payment processing).

**Parent control:** Parents can review, correct, or delete their child's data at any time through the parental dashboard or by contacting us. **Delete data:** Request deletion at support@dinoai.ai—we aim to respond within 15 business days for children's data requests.

## For Parents: What You Need to Know About Your Child's Privacy

Dino English is an English-learning app for children. If your child is under 13 and lives in the United States, US federal law — the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) — gives you specific rights over how we handle their information. This section explains those rights in plain language. The full legal notice follows below.

### What information do we collect from your child?

When your child uses the App, we collect:

- **Your information (parent/guardian):** your name, email address, country, and payment details if you subscribe to a paid plan.
- **Your child's profile:** a display name or username, and an age range or year of birth. We do not require your child's full date of birth.
- **Learning activity:** lesson progress, quiz answers, scores, and time spent on each activity — so the App can personalize lessons to your child's level.
- **Course and class activity:** course selections, course list activity, class entry and completion status, lesson page activity, button interactions inside and outside class, and teacher or AI tutor character selections.
- **Voice recordings:** when your child uses pronunciation practice features, their voice is recorded and analyzed to give feedback on pronunciation. We do not store these voice recordings — the audio is processed in real time and discarded immediately after analysis.
- **AI tutor conversations:** text and voice exchanges with the App's AI tutor characters. These are kept for up to [90] days and then deleted.
- **Feature interaction data:** information about how your child uses App features, such as which learning path, lesson, practice activity, or page they use, retry patterns, response latency, and feature taps. We use this to run the App, personalize learning, fix errors, and improve usability.
- **Device information:** device model, operating system, and app version — used to keep the App running smoothly and securely.

We collect only what we need to run the educational features. We do not ask for your child's full name, home address, phone number, or school.

If we make available paid subscriptions, gamified learning, rewards, learning games, Dino Agent, learning reports, push notifications, check-in streaks, rankings, or other future features, we will collect only the information reasonably needed to provide those features. We will not use your child's information for targeted advertising, and we will not sell your child's information.

### Why do we collect it?

To teach English. Specifically: to deliver lessons, score pronunciation, adapt the difficulty to your child's progress, and keep the App secure. We do not use your child's information for advertising — targeted or otherwise.

### Who can see it?

- **Our technology partners** — companies that help us run the App (cloud servers, speech recognition, payment processing). They are contractually required to use the data only to operate the App for us and may not use it for their own purposes.
- **No advertisers or data brokers.** We do not sell your child's information. We do not share it with advertising networks.
- **Law enforcement,** if required by law.

### What can you do as a parent?

You have the right to:

| What you can do | How |
|---|---|
| Review what we have collected about your child | Email us at support@dinoai.ai with subject line "COPPA — Review Request" |
| Correct inaccurate information | Email us at support@dinoai.ai with subject line "COPPA — Correction Request" |
| Delete your child's information | Email us at support@dinoai.ai with subject line "COPPA — Deletion Request" — we aim to respond within 15 business days |
| Stop further collection — withdraw your consent | Email us at support@dinoai.ai — we will stop collecting new data; your child's access to the App may end |

We will verify your identity before acting on any request. We will never delete information we are legally required to keep.

### What happens if you withdraw consent?

We stop collecting your child's data. Because the App needs basic information to work, your child may no longer be able to use the App after you withdraw consent.

### Do we use your child's information to train AI models?

Only if you separately opt in. We will ask for your specific consent before using your child's learning data to improve our AI. You can opt out at any time without affecting your child's access to the App. If you have not opted in, your child's data is not used for AI training.

### What is "personal information"?

Personal information is any information that could be used to identify you. For example: your name, the sound of your voice, or your learning scores are personal information. Information that cannot identify you — for example, the total number of children who completed a lesson — is not personal information.

### What information do we collect about you?

Here is a simple list of the information Dino English collects:

**From your parent or guardian when they set up your account**

- Their name and email address
- The country you live in
- Payment details if they subscribe to a paid plan (your payment details are never shared with us — they go straight to Apple or Google)

**From your profile**

- A display name or username you choose
- Your age range or year of birth (not your full date of birth)

**When you use the App**

| What you do in the App | What we collect |
|---|---|
| Choose a course or teacher | The course you choose, the teacher or AI tutor character you pick, and whether you enter or finish the class |

- Your full name (only a display name)
- Your home address
- Your phone number
- Your photo
- Where you are right now (we do not track your location)
- Information about what you do on other apps or websites

## 1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Data

We collect data that you actively provide when using the Services, as well as data automatically generated during your use or interaction with the Services, through the following methods:

**Account Registration.** When a parent or guardian registers an account, we collect information necessary to create and manage the account, including: your name, email address, country, and payment information if you subscribe to paid features. For the child's profile, we collect a display name or username and an age range or year of birth (not the full date of birth). We do not require the child's full date of birth unless needed to comply with applicable age-verification law.

**AI-Powered Learning Services.** The App provides interactive English-learning features powered by AI technology. When your child uses the App, we collect and process the following types of content for the purpose of delivering learning features: (a) Learning Activity Data: lesson progress, scores, completion rates, quiz responses, time-on-task, and session duration; (b) AI Tutor Dialogue: text and voice exchanges with AI tutor characters within the App, including written responses to AI prompts; (c) Engagement Signals: response latency and help-seeking behavior, used to adapt the learning experience.

**Voice and Audio Data.** The App includes voice-based features for pronunciation assessment and oral practice. When your child uses these features, we collect the audio recording and process it using AI speech recognition to assess pronunciation, fluency, and intonation. Audio recordings are processed in real time and are not stored; the audio is discarded immediately after scoring is complete. You may disable voice features at any time in the App settings.

**Course, Class, and Teacher Selection Features.** The App may provide course lists, class pages, pre-class teacher or AI tutor character selection, and in-class or out-of-class learning activities. When your child uses these features, we collect course selections, class entry and completion status, teacher or AI tutor character selections, lesson page activity, button interactions, scores, completion rates, quiz responses, time-on-task, retry patterns, response latency, and help-seeking behavior. We use this information to deliver courses, support the child's selected learning path, recommend the next lesson or activity, personalize difficulty, maintain product security, debug errors, and improve usability.

## 2. AI-Powered Features and How They Work

The App uses AI in several ways to support your child's English learning. This section explains how each feature works and what data it processes.

**Voice Recognition and Pronunciation Scoring.** When your child uses pronunciation practice features, their voice audio is processed by AI speech recognition models to assess pronunciation, fluency, and intonation. The audio is transmitted to our servers (or the servers of our AI model providers) for processing. Retention of audio follows the option selected under the "Voice and Audio Data" section above.

**Adaptive Learning Recommendations.** The App analyses your child's learning activity data (scores, progress, time-on-task) to personalize lesson sequences and adjust difficulty levels. This processing is advisory — it suggests a learning path, but the parent may manually adjust the child's program at any time. This does not constitute solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 22).

**Teacher Selection and Course Recommendations.** The App may use course activity, progress, scores, teacher or AI tutor character selections, response patterns, and prior lesson results to recommend a course, teacher, lesson, or learning activity. These recommendations are educational and advisory. They do not produce legal or similarly significant effects, and parents may adjust the child's learning path where the App makes such controls available.

**Dino Agent and Learning Scenario Support.** If we introduce Dino Agent or similar AI learning assistant features, the Agent may process text, voice, learning activity, task completion, and scenario-selection data to generate dialogue, recommend tasks, and support additional learning scenarios. Agent responses are AI-generated, may contain errors or omissions, and are provided for educational reference only.

**AI Tutor Dialogue.** When your child interacts with AI tutor characters, the text or voice exchanges are processed by AI language models to generate responses. Dialogue content is retained for up to [90] days after the session, then automatically deleted or anonymized. Content is not reviewed by human staff unless the parent submits a support request that requires it.

**Engagement Inference.** The App may use response patterns (such as response latency, retry patterns, and help-seeking behavior) to infer the child's comprehension or engagement level for the sole purpose of adapting the learning experience. This inference is used only within the App and is not shared with third parties or used for commercial purposes.

**AI Model Training.** See the "AI Model Training and Service Improvement" section above for our practices regarding the use of learner data for AI model training.
